**Project title**
Global Development Data Analytics | Python, SQL & Interactive Dashboard

**Project description**
Reproducible exploratory analysis of 1,704 country-year observations from the historical Gapminder dataset (142 countries; 1952–2007). The project validates data quality; examines population-weighted life expectancy across regions; investigates its cross-country association with GDP per capita; runs SQL queries in SQLite; and presents results in an interactive HTML dashboard. Findings are historical and descriptive, not causal.
